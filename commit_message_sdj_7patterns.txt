feat(sdj): Implement 7-Pattern Psychometric Report Overhaul

BREAKING: None (backward compatible - legacy reports still work)

## Summary
Complete modernization of SDJ psychometric reports with new 7-pattern analysis framework,
Arabic-first design, and enhanced visualizations.

## New Features

### 7-Pattern Scoring System
- Maps 24 sub-dimensions to 7 major patterns:
  1. الأنماط الشخصية (Personality Patterns)
  2. القدرات المعرفية والعقلية (Cognitive & Mental)
  3. الأنماط النفسية (Psychological Patterns)
  4. الأنماط السلوكية (Behavioral Patterns)
  5. الأنماط العددية والمنطقية (Numerical & Logical)
  6. الأنماط القيادية والتنظيمية (Leadership & Organizational)
  7. الاستعدادات المهنية العامة (Professional Readiness)
- Deterministic T-score aggregation
- Band classification (Weak <40, Average 40-54.9, Excellent ≥55)

### Modern Arabic PDF Report (3 pages)
- **Page 1**: Centered logo (SAITEST-ICON.png) + participant info + 7-pattern summary
- **Page 2**: Heptagon radar chart + horizontal bar chart for sub-dimensions
- **Page 3**: Weak areas table with Arabic course recommendations

### Heptagon Radar Chart
- True 7-axis polar chart (vector rendering with SkiaSharp)
- Arabic labels with proper RTL support
- Color-coded rings at T-score thresholds (40, 55, 70)
- Title: "الخريطة النفسية السباعية"

### Course Recommendations Engine
- Rule-based mapping (no AI/ML dependencies)
- 2-3 Arabic courses per weak sub-dimension
- Covers all 24 sub-dimensions with duration estimates
